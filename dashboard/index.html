What the simulation expects

From 2,000 simulated two-year paths of this fleet: 15 accounts with a $3,000 drawdown and 5 with $2,500, 1 MNQ each, all copying the same trades, $3,000 target, $1,000 payouts with no consistency rule, $130 per evaluation. “Unseen year” resamples only the year the strategy was not chosen on. “Full two years” also includes the year it was chosen on, so treat it as optimistic.

Assumptions and limits
  • Payouts count as $1,000 to you. Profit splits, activation fees and monthly fees count only if you log them above.
  • The strategy rests on two years of Nasdaq-100 data, one of them unseen. It has not been tested in a bear market like 2022.
  • Every account takes the same trades, so good and bad months hit the whole fleet together.
  • The expectations use the backtest’s fill prices. In a test of the live bot against a simulated broker that could only fill on one-minute bars, slippage cut profit by about 40%. Real fills checked every few seconds should land between the two, so treat these figures as the upper end.
  • The drawdown floor here is a simple model of an end-of-day trailing drawdown. Your firm’s dashboard is the authority.
  • Anyone who can open this dashboard can read the accounts and the log. Only its editors can save changes.
